Privacy

The short version: the game keeps your progress on your device, and nothing about you leaves it.

What’s stored

Your browser’s local storage holds which levels you’ve beaten, your death counts and best times, and your settings (sound, music, vibration, controls). It stays on this device, is never sent anywhere, and there are no accounts.

To delete it, use Settings → Reset progress, or clear this site’s data in your browser.

What isn’t

No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no cookies. The game doesn’t know who you are, and it doesn’t want to. It just wants you to fall in that hole again.

Other services

  • Hosting: the game is served by Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operations.
  • Fonts: the Bungee and Rubik fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees a request from your browser when the page first loads.

After the first visit, the game is cached on your device and works offline.
